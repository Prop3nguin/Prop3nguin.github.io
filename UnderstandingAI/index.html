Understanding AI

Education

In the modern day, Artificial intelligence has come into the spotlight leveraged by large companies hoping to multiply their wealth. While nothing is inherently wrong with this, some people may be harmed by AI and won't end up realizing it until later.

On January 29th 2026, Anthropic the company behind Claude the AI, published a study that compared the comprehension a group of developers had of a new programming library after they were given a project to complete with it. Only some were allowed access to AI.

Fig. 1. Judy Hanwen, "How AI Impacts Skill Formation", How AI Impacts Skill Formation, January 26 2026, https://www.anthropic.com/research/AI-assistance-coding-skills

Figure 1 is a graph that shows the average time and comprehension between two groups of developers unfamiliar with the library that they were using. On average, we can see that the group without AI scored higher and wasn’t meaningfully behind. In the end the average effect of AI use is getting a 35 minute project done 2 minutes faster, and failing the comprehension test associated with the project.

Fig. 1. Judy Hanwen, "How AI Impacts Skill Formation", How AI Impacts Skill Formation, January 26 2026, https://www.anthropic.com/research/AI-assistance-coding-skills

Figure 2 is a graph that takes into account smaller averages of this 57 developer group. What this means is that within the group that used AI, the average groups were made even smaller to account for the different ways the developers used AI. This graph shows that just because you used AI for a project, it doesn’t guarantee that you can’t comprehend it. Some developers used AI for an initial generation of code and then asked it to explain the code line-by-line. They had a high average comprehension score.

Effect on jobs

AI has been used in many entry-level positions to replace the workers that filled those roles before. This creates a lack of entry-level positions for people looking to join the workforce. This kind of job-loss is especially noticeable in the programming and software development department, where the few people who might get hired, used AI in most, if not all of their projects that they can’t actually understand themselves.

Misinformation & Trust

Fig. 3. Samuel Bestvater, "Line chart with 1 series", How Much of the Internet Is Written With AI?, August 20th 2026, https://www.pewresearch.org/data-labs/2026/08/20/how-much-of-the-internet-is-written-with-ai/

Recently, AI videos and images have become so real-looking that they are nearly indiscernible from actual footage. This is going to create problems with the trust of information, since it’s becoming easier and easier to fabricate evidence and trick a number of people (possibly a jury) with this false, detailed information.

The Future

Many people theorise about the rise of AI and paint it to be an inherently bad thing, and while it is completely possible, it’s also possible that AI becomes something good for the future. It can be a tool that can help us push the boundaries of technology. The future is fickle, and can seem to change on a whim; what we take as fact today may be proven false tomorrow. The best course of action is to hope for the best, and prepare for the worst.
