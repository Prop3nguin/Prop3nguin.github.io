Understanding AI

Education

In the modern day, Artificial intelligence has come into the spotlight leveraged by large companies hoping to multiply their wealth. While nothing is inherently wrong with this, some people may be harmed by AI and won't end up realizing it until later.

On January 29th 2026, Anthropic the company behind Claude the AI, published a study that compared the comprehension a group of developers had of a new programming library after they were given a project to complete with it. Only some were allowed access to AI.

Fig 1. This is a WIP credit

Figure 1 is a graph that shows the average time and comprehension between two groups of developers unfamiliar with the library that they were using. On average, we can see that the group without AI scored higher and wasn’t meaningfully behind. In the end the average effect of AI use is getting a 35 minute project done 2 minutes faster, and failing the comprehension test associated with the project.

Fig 2. This is a WIP credit

Figure 2 is a graph that takes into account smaller averages of this 57 developer group. What this means is that within the group that used AI, the average groups were made even smaller to account for the different ways the developers used AI. This graph shows that just because you used AI for a project, it doesn’t guarantee that you can’t comprehend it. Some developers used AI for an initial generation of code and then asked it to explain the code line-by-line. They had a high average comprehension score.
